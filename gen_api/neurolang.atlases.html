neurolang.atlases#

Atlases: a label image, its affine, a label table and a space.

An Atlas is what .load_atlas, add_atlas_set and the atlases: of an engine load: the regions of the relation name(label, region) of the engine, the centres of their voxels, and what the engine tracts indexes streamlines by. This module owns the loaders (a FreeSurfer colour LUT, a BIDS dseg.tsv, a TSV, a CSV, a nilearn fetcher, the fetchers of the atlases of the engines), the normalisation of label names, the inference of the hemisphere of a label, the space tag and the content hash.

It needs numpy, pandas and nibabel only, and nilearn when an atlas is fetched, so that the core of the engine tracts may import it: it imports no other module of NeuroLang but neurolang.utils.nilearn_compat, lazily, to fetch (.importlinter).

Module Attributes

Space

A free-form tag naming a coordinate space, e.g. "MNI152NLin2009cAsym" or "subject:100307".

HEMISPHERES

The hemisphere of a label.

HEMISPHERE_RULES

The default, ordered rules; the first that matches wins: FreeSurfer Left-/Right-, ctx-lh-/ctx-rh- and a2009s ctx_lh_/ctx_rh_ (ctx lh `` as :func:`label_name` writes them), the suffixes ``_L/_R (AAL), lh./rh., .left/.right, the prefixes Left ``/``Right `` (Harvard-Oxford) and ``L ``/``R `` (nilearn's Destrieux), and the suffixes ``-lh/-rh (Tian's subcortex), `` lh``/`` rh`` as label_name() writes them, and Schaefer's 17Networks_LH_/ _RH_ (``17Networks LH ``).

FREESURFER_LUT_URL

FreeSurfer's colours of its labels, those of the Destrieux atlas (aparc.a2009s, ctx_lh_G_and_S_frontomargin...) among them.

DESTRIEUX_TO_MNI152

The millimetres of nilearn's Destrieux atlas to those of the MNI152 template: derived from FreeSurfer's fsaverage, it is 1.3 to 2.6% smaller than the template and off by about a millimetre.

TIAN_COLOURS_URL

The label files of the authors with the colours of the regions: those of the atlases of the cortex and the subcortex together.

FETCHERS

The fetchers of the deterministic atlases of the engines, by the kind of .load_atlas: each returns maps (a path) and labels, as nilearn's fetchers.

A deterministic atlas: label volume + affine + label table + space.

Parameters:
namestr

The atlas’s identifier, unique in an engine.

labelsnumpy.ndarray

3D volume of non-negative integer labels (floats with integral values are accepted); background marks voxels outside any region.

affinenumpy.ndarray

4x4, voxel indices (ijk) to the space’s world coordinates in mm (RAS+, as nibabel): the affine maps an index to the centre of its voxel.

tablepandas.DataFrame

Columns id, name and optionally hemi (left, right, none or empty). Ids of the table absent from the volume are dropped, with a warning; ids of the volume absent from the table (other than the background) are no region, with a warning (unlisted_labels).

spaceSpace

The space tag, "unspecified" by default.

backgroundint

The label that means “no region” (default 0): it has no region and no point, but keeps its row of the table if it has one.

hemisphere_rulesSequence[HemisphereRule]

Rules to infer the hemisphere from the name, where the table does not say it. Defaults to HEMISPHERE_RULES; () infers nothing.

kindstr, optional

How it was loaded: the kind of .load_atlas (freesurfer, destrieux…; nilearn:<fetcher> for a nilearn fetcher).

imageoptional

The nibabel image the volume was read from, kept as image.

Notes

self.table is the resolved label table: columns id (int32), name, hemi, by id, for the ids of the table in the volume. The hemisphere of a label is the hemi of the table, if not empty, else the inference by hemisphere_rules (hemispheres()).

hemispheres(hints=None)#

The hemisphere of each label of table, in its order.

It is, in order of precedence: the hemi of the label table, if not empty; the hint for its name, if any and not empty; the inference by the hemisphere rules. A warning names each label for which two of these disagree (the inference counting only when it is not none); with hints, only those where a hint is one of them.

Parameters:
hintsdict, optional

A label name -> left, right, none or "", e.g. the hemi of a term mapping of the engine tracts.
